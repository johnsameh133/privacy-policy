Privacy Policy for AI Bot

Effective Date: April 18, 2025

Thank you for using AI Bot. Your privacy is important to us.

1. What Data We Collect

When you interact with AI Bot on Messenger, we may collect the following information:

  • User name

2. How We Use Data

We use data to:

  • Understand and respond to User messages

We do not sell, rent, or misuse your personal data. We only share your messages with OpenAI’s API to generate responses — and nothing else.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect User information. Your data is stored securely and only accessible for necessary processing.

5. Your Rights

To do this, simply contact us.
